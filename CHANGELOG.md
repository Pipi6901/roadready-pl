## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-23

RoadReady PL helps learner drivers prepare for the Polish theory exam with the ministry's published questions and a mock exam that follows the real rules.

### Added

- 3,518 questions from the Ministry of Infrastructure's published catalogue, sorted into 23 sections, many with pictures or video clips that play on their own.
- Choose your licence category (AM, A1, A2, A, B, C or D) and get only the questions that apply to it.
- A mock exam with the Polish rules: 20 basic and 12 specialist questions, 25 minutes, a timer on every question, no going back, and 68 of 74 points to pass.
- Read questions in Polish, English, German or Ukrainian, with Russian, Spanish or Turkish available as a second line underneath.
- The whole app, including section names, works in seven languages.
- Lessons of 10 questions are free and unlimited; Pro adds the mock exam and a review of the questions you got wrong.
- Save any question to come back to it later, and keep a daily streak with achievements.
